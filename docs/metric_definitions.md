# 📘 Metric Definitions

## Overview

This document defines the business metrics used throughout the E-Commerce Intelligence Platform.

Each metric has a standardized business definition to ensure consistency across SQL queries, analytics marts, and Power BI dashboards.

The metrics documented here serve as the single source of truth for business reporting.

---

# Metric Categories

The platform organizes business metrics into six analytical domains:

- Executive Metrics
- Customer Metrics
- Product Metrics
- Seller Metrics
- Delivery Metrics
- Revenue Metrics

---

# 📊 Executive Metrics

## Total Revenue

### Definition

Total monetary value generated from completed marketplace sales.

### Business Purpose

Measures overall marketplace performance.

### Used In

- Executive Dashboard
- Revenue Analytics

---

## Total Orders

### Definition

Total number of completed customer orders.

### Business Purpose

Measures marketplace transaction volume.

### Used In

- Executive Dashboard
- Revenue Analytics

---

## Average Order Value (AOV)

### Definition

Average revenue generated per order.

### Business Purpose

Evaluates customer spending behavior.

### Used In

- Executive Dashboard
- Revenue Dashboard

---

## Total Freight Cost

### Definition

Total shipping cost associated with marketplace orders.

### Business Purpose

Monitors logistics expenditure.

### Used In

- Revenue Dashboard

---

# 👥 Customer Metrics

## Total Customers

### Definition

Total unique customers within the analytical dataset.

---

## Repeat Customers

### Definition

Customers who have placed more than one order.

---

## Repeat Customer Percentage

### Definition

Percentage of customers who have made multiple purchases.

---

## Average Customer Lifetime

### Definition

Average duration between a customer's first and last purchase.

---

# 📦 Product Metrics

## Total Products

### Definition

Total products available within the analytical dataset.

---

## Product Categories

### Definition

Number of unique product categories.

---

## High Image Products

### Definition

Products classified as having high-quality image availability.

---

## Rich Description Products

### Definition

Products containing complete product descriptions.

---

# 🏪 Seller Metrics

## Total Sellers

### Definition

Total active marketplace sellers.

---

## Large Sellers

### Definition

Sellers classified as high-volume sellers based on marketplace activity.

---

## Medium Sellers

### Definition

Sellers classified as medium-volume marketplace sellers.

---

## Average Products per Seller

### Definition

Average number of products managed by each seller.

---

# 🚚 Delivery Metrics

## Average Delivery Days

### Definition

Average number of days required to complete order delivery.

---

## Average Delivery Delay

### Definition

Average delay between estimated and actual delivery dates.

---

## Late Delivery Rate

### Definition

Percentage of deliveries completed after the promised delivery date.

---

# 💰 Revenue Metrics

## Revenue by Category

### Definition

Revenue contribution of each product category.

---

## Revenue by Seller

### Definition

Revenue generated by individual sellers.

---

## Monthly Revenue

### Definition

Revenue aggregated by purchase month.

---

## Quarterly Revenue

### Definition

Revenue aggregated by business quarter.

---

# Business Rules

The following business rules are applied consistently throughout the project:

- Revenue is calculated from completed marketplace transactions.
- Customer metrics are based on unique customer identifiers.
- Product metrics are aggregated at the product category level where applicable.
- Seller metrics are calculated using seller-level aggregations.
- Delivery metrics compare actual delivery dates against estimated delivery dates.
- KPI definitions remain consistent across SQL analytics marts and Power BI dashboards.

---

# Metric Usage

These standardized metrics are consumed by:

- SQL Analytics Layer
- Analytics Marts
- Power BI Dashboards
- Executive Reporting
- Business Performance Monitoring

Maintaining centralized metric definitions ensures consistent business reporting across all analytical modules.
